Privacy Policy

Mechanical Tutor | Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Mechanical Tutor, an educational ChatGPT plugin published by Ahmed Z. Sohail, handles information.

2. Information Used

Mechanical Tutor may use information that learners voluntarily provide in their ChatGPT conversations, such as learning goals, educational background, answers to questions and learning progress. This information helps personalise tutoring.

3. Data Collection and Storage

Mechanical Tutor is a skills-based plugin. It does not operate its own external server or database and does not independently collect or store learners' ChatGPT conversations.

Conversations are processed by OpenAI under its applicable privacy policies, terms and account settings.

4. Sharing

Mechanical Tutor does not independently sell or share learner information with advertisers or other third parties. OpenAI's handling of conversation data is governed by OpenAI's own policies.

5. Public Support Requests

If learners contact support through GitHub Issues, their posts may be publicly visible. Avoid including passwords, private information or sensitive personal details.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Mechanical Tutor does not maintain a separate learner database. ChatGPT conversation history and related data are subject to OpenAI's applicable retention and deletion policies. GitHub support posts are subject to GitHub's policies.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when the plugin's functionality or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through GitHub Issues .
