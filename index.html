What is Mechanical Tutor?

Mechanical Tutor adapts its teaching to each learner. Instead of simply giving answers, it identifies knowledge gaps, explains concepts in manageable steps and checks understanding before moving forward.

What can it do?

Personalised lessons

Lessons adapt to your current understanding and learning needs.

Guided practice

Practise questions and receive feedback that helps you improve.

Progress tracking

Keep track of topics covered, strengths and areas to improve.

Mastery-based learning

Build a solid understanding before moving on to harder material.
